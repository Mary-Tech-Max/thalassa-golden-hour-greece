Why Greece?

Greece is a sun-soaked country in south-east Europe where history and holiday meet. It is the birthplace of democracy, the Olympic Games and some of the world's greatest myths. Today it is famous for its snow-white villages, blue-domed chapels, and thousands of islands scattered across the Aegean and Ionian seas.

Whether you love history, beaches, food or hiking, Greece has a corner that feels made just for you.
