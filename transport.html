Getting Around Greece

Modes of transport and sample schedule times

⛴️ Ferry: the classic island link

Ferries leave from the ports of Piraeus, Rafina and Lavrio near Athens. High-speed boats are faster, while conventional ferries are cheaper and more relaxed (and you can sit on deck!). Book ahead in July and August.

RouteTypical departureJourney timeType
Piraeus → Santorini07:25 / 07:305–8 hoursHigh-speed / conventional
Piraeus → Mykonos07:00 / 08:002.5–5.5 hoursHigh-speed / conventional

Sample summer times for planning only. Ferry timetables change every season, so always confirm with the operator before you travel.

✈️ Domestic flights: save time

Short flights from Athens International Airport (ATH) to the islands take under an hour. They are great when you only have a few days, and the views from the window are free!

RouteFlights in summerFlight time
Athens → SantoriniSeveral each day (morning to evening)About 45–55 min
Athens → MykonosSeveral each dayAbout 40–50 min
Athens → Heraklion (Crete)Many each dayAbout 55–60 min
Athens → ThessalonikiMany each dayAbout 55 min

Arrive at the airport about 90 minutes before a domestic flight. Check airline websites for exact times.

🚇 Metro & bus in Athens

Athens has a clean, easy-to-use metro. Line 3 (blue line) connects the airport to the city centre, and the X95 bus runs between the centre and the airport around the clock.

💡 Quick tips

  • Book ferries early in summer and take a motion-sickness tablet if you're sensitive.
  • On islands you can rent a scooter, an ATV or use local buses.
  • Keep a day or two of buffer before an international flight in case of strikes or bad weather.
